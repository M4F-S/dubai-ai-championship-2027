Formal Institutional Undertaking & Core Commitment
"We hereby confirm our formal commitment to pilot the Taryaq v0 sovereign clinical AI decision support system in Q1 2027 (February 1 – March 15, 2027) in shadow advisory mode, and to share anonymised evaluation results for the Dubai AI Agents Championship 2026–2027."
1. Clinical & Operational Scope

Prime Healthcare Group LLC hereby engages Taryaq Sovereign Clinical AI Systems to deploy and evaluate the Taryaq v0 decision-support system at the Jumeirah Medical Center and Community Pharmacy:

  • Evaluation Volume: 500 consecutive outpatient prescriptions across high-risk chronic polypharmacy cohorts (Internal Medicine, Cardiology, Nephrology).
  • Billing Claim Scrubbing: 500 pre-submission electronic claim XML payloads destined for the Dubai Health Post Office (eClaimLink / DHPO).
  • Evaluation Period: Six-week prospective window during Q1 2027 (February 1 through March 15, 2027).
2. Human-in-the-Loop & Safety Protocol

In full alignment with DHA Clinical Governance and Health Regulation Sector guidelines:

  • Shadow Advisory Mode: Taryaq runs strictly non-interventional; it possesses zero autonomous authority to alter orders or dispense drugs.
  • Pharmacist Primacy: The licensed clinical pharmacist retains sole, unfettered legal authority over all medication dispensing and clinical interventions.
  • Immutable Audit: Every warning, flag, and human clinician override is committed to an append-only, SHA-256 cryptographic audit ledger (taryaq_audit.jsonl).
3. Statutory Sovereignty & DHA Regulatory Guarantees

UAE Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 (Health ICT Law, Art. 13): 100% on-premises deployment on a dedicated physical appliance (Minisforum UM790 Pro mini-PC) inside the clinic server room. Air-gapped execution over local POSIX domain sockets (AF_UNIX) with zero cloud telemetry, zero WAN routing, and zero third-party external data transmission.

DHA Standards Notice SN 03/2019 (Claim Cycle Standardisation): Automated pre-submission validation against statutory initial submission deadlines (15th of the following month) and 21-day Remittance Advice resubmission rules, calculating and preventing the statutory 0.03% per calendar day late delay penalty (§3.8.4, §3.9.3).

Data Rights & Championship Reporting: All operational data shall be anonymised in compliance with DHA guidelines. Aggregated statistical findings (contraindication detection sensitivity, sub-millisecond latency benchmarks, and avoided late penalties in AED) are authorized for presentation to the Technical Jury of the Dubai AI Agents Championship 2026–2027.
