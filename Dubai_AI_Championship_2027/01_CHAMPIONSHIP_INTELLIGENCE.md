# 1\. Championship Intelligence & Judging Expectations (DCDE)

## 1.1 Overview & Strategic Thesis

Launched on October 7, 2026, by H.H. Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, Crown Prince of Dubai, Deputy Prime Minister, and Minister of Defence of the UAE, the **Create AI Agents Championship** is organized by the **Dubai Chamber of Digital Economy (DCDE)** with a prize pool exceeding **AED 2.5 million (\$750,000+ USD)**. It formally retires the three-year *Create Apps Championship* cycle, shifting Dubai's venture creation funnel from mobile apps to autonomous, tool-executing agentic systems.

An analysis of historical competition cohorts reveals a stark structural vulnerability among typical entrants:

* Across three editions of the predecessor championship (15,564 applications from 153 countries), **only 11% (\~1,700 entrants) built functional prototypes**, and **fewer than 1% (140+ ventures) achieved production launch**.  
* Accelerated by DCDE's partnership with no-code platform *Lovable* (providing Dubai Chambers-funded platform credits) and introductory *n8n* and *LangChain* bootcamps, **over 90% of the 2026–2027 applicant pool will consist of brittle Python or no-code prompt-wrappers** reliant on third-party American or Chinese cloud APIs.  
* These wrapper prototypes fail on the two exact dimensions that the championship and UAE enterprise buyers scrutinize most heavily: **multi-turn deterministic execution reliability** and **strict sovereign data protection**.

## 1.3 Explicit and Implicit Judging Criteria

Cross-referencing the official terms, workshop curricula, and DCDE's Business Matching qualification rubrics exposes the four core evaluation gates:

1. **"Acting, Not Chatting" (Verifiable Multi-Step Tool Execution):** Evaluators inspect whether the system queries external states, executes code or API transactions, modifies records deterministically, and verifies the resulting world state.  
2. **Uncompromising Data Protection & Sovereignty:** The official rule mandates: *"The solution must protect personal, confidential and organizational data and prevent information leakage."* Entrants using real customer or corporate records in demonstration videos risk immediate disqualification. Winning entries must operate over synthetic test datasets while proving architectural isolation against prompt injection and unauthorized data exfiltration.  
3. **Human-in-the-Loop Governance & Observability:** Evaluators require observable audit logs, deterministic rollback controls, and explicit human approval checkpoints where mistakes carry financial, medical, or legal liability.  
4. **Quantifiable Unit Economics & B2B/B2G Procurement Readiness:** Because prize disbursements require a Dubai trade license and feed directly into DCDE's **Business Matching programme** (where 11 Dubai-based enterprises have already pledged to integrate Agentic AI and 14,000 private-sector firms are slated for Agentic AI training), judges evaluate total operating cost at expected UAE workload volumes and measurable cycle-time reduction.

AI-led digital transformation is tasked with generating **AED 100 billion annually** and increasing overall economic productivity by **50%**.

  1. Best application of AI in strategic sectors (finance, healthcare, logistics, transport, real estate, energy utilities).  
  2. Global regulatory and governance sandbox for AI (*Sandbox Dubai*).  
  3. Highest adoption of AI in government work (22 Chief AI Officers appointed across government entities; RTA AI Strategy 2030 with 81 projects cutting travel time by 30% and transport costs by 44%; DEWA smart-grid forecasting achieving \>24% energy savings).  
  4. Optimal environment for AI companies and talent (empowering 1 million learners with AI skills by 2027; 100% renewable-powered AI data centers by 2033).
