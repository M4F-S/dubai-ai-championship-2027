# 3\. Skeptical Audit & Forking Strategy (`Qala` and `Nibras`)

## 3.1 Executive Verdict: Why We Must Build *Over* the C99 Engines, Not Rewrite Them

`Belya` and `Almaz` possess an **85%+ technical advantage** over the competition, but in their raw form they suffer from a **20% pitch-market fit** for a Dubai Chambers jury.

Think of `Belya` and `Almaz` as two Formula 1 engines sitting on a workbench. The judges in Dubai—government ministers, Chief AI Officers, corporate buyers, and VCs—are fleet buyers, not C systems programmers. If you wheel a bare engine onto the stage and show terminal logs while another team drives out a slow Python/n8n go-kart wrapped in a shiny fiberglass dashboard solving a Dubai customs or banking problem, **the go-kart will win**.

**The Solution:** Keep `Belya` and `Almaz` 100% pure in their original repositories. Create two clean competition forks—**`Qala`** and **`Nibras`**—and bolt a thin **Enterprise, Policy & Visual Chassis** on top of the existing C99 engines.
