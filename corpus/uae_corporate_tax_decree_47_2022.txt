[SECTION: Standard Rates]
[TITLE: Corporate Tax Standard Rates and Statutory Brackets]
Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 on the Taxation of Corporations and Businesses establishes standard corporate tax rates: Standard rate: 0% up to AED 375,000; 9% on taxable net profit exceeding AED 375,000.

[SECTION: Small Business Relief]
[TITLE: Small Business Relief Ministerial Decision No. 73 of 2023]
Small Business Relief (Ministerial Decision No. 73 of 2023 under Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022) for revenue <= AED 3,000,000: Eligible resident taxable persons may elect for Small Business Relief, treating taxable income as zero for that period.
