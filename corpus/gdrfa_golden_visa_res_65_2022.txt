[SECTION: Real Estate Investor]
[TITLE: Golden Visa Real Estate Investor Category Requirements]
Cabinet Resolution No. 65 of 2022 Real Estate Investor category: Minimum investment AED 2,000,000 (mortgaged allowed with bank NOC confirming AED 2M paid). Grants a 10-year renewable residence visa for real estate investors in Dubai and UAE.

[SECTION: Skilled Professional]
[TITLE: Golden Visa Skilled Professional Category Requirements]
Cabinet Resolution No. 65 of 2022 Skilled Professional category: Valid employment contract, first/second professional level per MOHRE, minimum salary AED 30,000/month, Bachelor's degree or equivalent. Grants a 10-year renewable Golden Visa for skilled professionals.
