[SECTION: Electricity Slabs]
[TITLE: Electricity Residential Tariff Slabs and Fuel Surcharge]
Dubai Electricity and Water Authority (DEWA) residential electricity tariff slabs: Slab 1: 0-2000 kWh @ 23 fils; Slab 2: 2001-4000 kWh @ 28 fils; Slab 3: 4001-6000 kWh @ 32 fils; Slab 4: >6000 kWh @ 38 fils. Fuel surcharge 5 fils/kWh across all electricity consumption.

[SECTION: Water Slabs]
[TITLE: Water Residential Tariff Slabs and Fuel Surcharge]
DEWA residential water tariff slabs: Slab 1: 0-6000 IG @ 3.5 fils; Slab 2: 6001-12000 IG @ 4.0 fils; Slab 3: >12000 IG @ 4.6 fils. Fuel surcharge 0.6 fils/IG across all water consumption.
